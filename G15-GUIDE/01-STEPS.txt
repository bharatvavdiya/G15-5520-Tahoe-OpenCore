WHAT THIS LAPTOP CAN AND CANNOT DO IN macOS (checked against Apple and WhateverXe docs)
  - Iris Xe: Metal 2 acceleration, the level Apple's Iris Xe driver provides.
    Metal 3 and Metal 4 are not possible: Metal 4 needs Apple silicon (M1 or newer),
    and Apple never made a Metal 3 driver for Iris Xe.
  - RTX 3050: no macOS driver exists for RTX 20/30/40 cards. Best is to switch it off
    in macOS (mode D); it keeps working fully in Windows. The HDMI port is wired to
    the RTX, so HDMI will not work in macOS.
  - CPU: all 12 cores run with Apple's power management. 16 threads (Hyper-Threading
    on) needs the CPU topology kext that caused the random start hangs, so it stays
    off for now.
  - WhateverXe lists your exact chip (80-EU Alder Lake-H) and its built-in screen as
    "expected to work, not yet tested on hardware".
